Haverfordwest Havock

A cosy pixel-art town builder set in Haverfordwest, Pembrokeshire. Made for family and friends.

Support

If something is wrong, or you have an idea, email havock@medland.io. Please say which phone or tablet you use and what you were doing when the problem happened.

How to play

Tap Start New Game, watch or skip the opening, then follow the short tour: build Jill's house, lay a road to the bridge, and open a shop. Families move in and more buildings unlock as the town grows. Your town saves itself on your device.

Privacy

The game collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
