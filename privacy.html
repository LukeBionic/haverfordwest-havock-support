Privacy Policy

Haverfordwest Havock for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. Last updated 8 November 2026.

The short version

The game collects no data about you. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics. It does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your saved town and your settings (for example music on or off) are stored only on your phone, tablet or computer. They are never sent anywhere. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Photos

If you choose to share a postcard of your town, the device's own share sheet handles it. The game only asks for permission to save to Photos if you pick "Save Image". It never reads your photo library.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
